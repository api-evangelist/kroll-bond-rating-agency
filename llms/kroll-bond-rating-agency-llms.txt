# Kroll Bond Rating Agency (KBRA)

> KBRA is a full-service credit rating agency founded in 2010 by Jules Kroll and headquartered in New York. It is registered with the U.S. SEC as a Nationally Recognized Statistical Rating Organization (NRSRO) and with ESMA and the UK FCA through its Irish and UK entities. KBRA has issued 96,000+ ratings covering $4.78 trillion of rated issuance across ABS, CMBS, RMBS, structured credit, corporates, financial institutions, funds, insurance, project finance/infrastructure, public finance, and sovereigns.

Important note for agents: **KBRA publishes no public REST/GraphQL API and no developer portal.** Machine access to KBRA data is a commercial, contract-gated arrangement delivered as bulk feeds (email, FTP/SFTP), through the Snowflake Marketplace, or via third-party platforms (Bloomberg, Aladdin, FactSet). There is no self-serve API key, no OpenAPI description, no SDKs, and no sandbox. Do not attempt to construct API calls against api.kbra.com — it fronts the website backend and returns 404 for all public paths.

## Data products and delivery

- [Analytics products overview](https://www.kbra.com/analytics/products/overview): The KBRA Analytics application family.
- [Ratings Feed](https://www.kbra.com/analytics/products/feeds): Enterprise ratings data covering 90,000+ public ratings. Issuer- and security-level ratings with rating actions (assigned, affirmed, upgraded, downgraded, withdrawn), keyed on market-standard identifiers (CUSIP, ISIN, LEI), updated daily, with audit-ready rating history. Delivery: direct from KBRA via email/FTP/SFTP with sector-level customization and flexible scheduling; via the Snowflake Marketplace; or through Bloomberg, Aladdin, and FactSet. A "Ratings Feed Specifications" technical sheet is published as a PDF for data managers.
- [Credit Intelligence](https://www.kbra.com/analytics/products/credit-intelligence): Benchmarking of private credit borrowers on adjusted, normalized financial metrics.
- [Direct Lending Deals (DLD)](https://www.kbra.com/analytics/products/dld): Monthly intelligence on US and European direct lending terms, structures, and pricing.
- [Premium](https://www.kbra.com/analytics/products/premium): Research, surveillance, and analytics across structured finance and private credit.
- [Credit Profile (KCP)](https://www.kbra.com/analytics/products/kcp): Loan-level surveillance and analytics for securitized CRE debt.
- [Financial Intelligence (KFI)](https://www.kbra.com/analytics/products/kfi): Benchmarking and risk assessment across 10,000+ U.S. banks and credit unions; delivered as a web application, an Excel add-in, and data feeds.
